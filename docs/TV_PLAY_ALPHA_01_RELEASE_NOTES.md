## Store listing copy

### App name

24Seven.FM TV

### Short description

Live 24Seven.FM radio for Android TV with Now Playing, queue, and sleep timer.

### Full description

Listen to supported public 24Seven.FM live stations on Android TV. Use your TV remote to switch stations,
view Now Playing information, browse the public queue and recently played tracks, and control playback through
Android's media controls. A sleep timer is available for listening sessions.

This alpha is designed for anonymous listening and does not include accounts, purchases, chat, requests, or
notifications. An internet connection is required; stream availability may vary.

## Internal-test release notes

First Android TV alpha: live listening across five public 24Seven.FM stations, D-pad station selection, Now
Playing artwork and metadata, public Queue and Recently Played views, system media controls, sleep timer, and
audio-reactive idle visualizers. Test on a supported Android TV device and report playback, remote-focus,
accessibility, or visualizer issues without including personal information or logs.
