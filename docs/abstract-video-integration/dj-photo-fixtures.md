# Full-width DJ ceiling rig

The overhead trusses now span the full screen. Twenty-eight shallow circular seven-LED pucks are distributed across them, with photographic faces, discreet mounts, and music-driven turning/tilt/color. Four larger projectors use photographic housings. Equipment sits behind the live haze, beams and washes. The wall logo is 15% smaller and lowered below the rig; the original logo artwork, booth, speakers and animated decks are preserved.
